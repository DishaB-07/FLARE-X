# NASA PSI citation verification — FLARE-X final release

**Review date:** 14 November 2026. **Source:** NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) and NASA programme pages.

## Interpretation

- **Six** PSI investigation pages have independently confirmed their exact PSI ID, title and investigation DOI in NASA PSI indexed content.
- The other **18** have official NASA programme or investigation *context* supporting the research themes/titles, but **their exact PSI landing-page DOIs remain unverified**. This is not a 24-of-24 DOI verification.
- The NASA PSI listing is JavaScript-rendered and could not be fully retrieved with the available web reader; opening most unindexed individual pages did not expose verifiable content.
- A related investigation link or programme page is **not** evidence of that investigation’s own DOI.
- No full papers, raw data or files from PSI were downloaded; the 24 local records are a captured catalogue snapshot, not the live repository total.

## Authoritative citation procedure

1. Open [NASA PSI investigation search](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/search?q=&data_source=psi&data_type=investigation).
2. Search the investigation title or PSI accession ID.
3. Open the investigation page and use **Cite this Investigation** to obtain its official citation/identifier.
4. Keep report/article DOIs separate from the **investigation DOI**; do not create DOI URLs from guesses.
5. Confirm the link works without login before making a claim in the NASA Space Apps submission.

## Caution about individual examples

- **PSI-117:** A NASA Science article mentioned the research and PSI-117, while describing data availability as forthcoming *at the time the article was written*. This does not prove the investigation has since been released or has a DOI.
- **PSI-62:** Official PSI classifies this as a **Ground Investigation** focused on modelling and comparison with BASS experiments; not a new spaceflight observation.
- **PSI-99/100 and PSI-26:** Related-record references in verified PSI pages are not independent verification of their own landing-page titles or DOIs.
